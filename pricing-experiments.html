Decision rules

  • Run one experiment at a time; change one variable per test.
  • Record the price, the window, and the exact conversion or revenue number.
  • Never extrapolate from a single flash sale; let the tracker accumulate.
  • Kill a test quickly if it does not move the number you care about.
