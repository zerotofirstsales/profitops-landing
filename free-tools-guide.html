Free Tools to Launch a Digital Product

You can launch a digital product with zero upfront budget. These categories cover every stage of the launch.

Storefront and payments

Use a storefront that charges only when you sell. Several platforms offer free shops with a small per-sale fee. Payhip and Ko-fi are popular starter options.

Design and covers

Use a free design tool to make a clean cover and simple graphics. Start with a solid background, one readable title, and one subheading.

File delivery

Store the deliverable in a simple cloud folder and link it from your product page. Keep the file name clear and check the download path yourself.

Analytics and search

Add lightweight analytics to your owned pages. Submit your page to search console so new pages can be indexed over time.
