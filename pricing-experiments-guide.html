Pricing Experiments Guide

Pricing is a guess until you test it. Run one small experiment at a time, record the result, and keep what improves sales.

Twelve low-cost tests

Anchor a price with a higher option. Offer a bundle. Time-box a launch price. Share a coupon. Try pay-what-you-want. Add a second tier. Rename the product. Give a free sample. Run a time-limited discount. Publish a VAT-inclusive price. Change the payment method shown. Add social proof.

How to run a test

Pick one variable. Change it. Run for a fixed window, at least a week when possible. Record the price, the result, and the decision: keep, kill, or retry. Never change two variables at once.
