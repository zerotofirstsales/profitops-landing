Email Swipes for a Digital Product Launch

Use these four short email templates when you launch a digital product to people who already opted in. Keep every email friendly, specific, and honest.

Announcement (3 days before)

Subject: A new tool to [solve problem]. Body: I built [product] to help you [solve problem]. It goes live on [date] at [price]. Reply to this email if you want the link first.

Launch day

Subject: It is live: [product]. Body: [Product] is available now at [link]. It includes [one strong benefit]. If it does not help you, refunds are easy.

Follow-up (48 hours later)

Subject: One thing to know about [product]. Body: People using [product] most often save the most time with [one feature]. Here is the link again: [link].

Final call (day 7)

Subject: Last reminder for [product]. Body: This launch price stays until [date]. After that it returns to [normal price]. Grab it now: [link].
